BAT Roles & Access Rights

Who signs in as what for acceptance testing, and exactly what each role can see and do. Every tester uses their own login on the role they will really do — nobody tests as the administrator, because a screen that works for the Group Admin can still be refused to the storekeeper, and finding that is the point.

Passwords. Each login starts with a temporary password that must be changed at the first sign-in, to at least 10 characters. Temporary passwords are handed to each tester privately — never in a group chat or email thread.

The access review

On 21 September 2026 every role was checked against what its work needs. The built-in roles used to be given whole modules, and each module grant had grown as screens were added — a Site Engineer could read every employee's salary. Each role now names only its screens, and three separations hold:

  • Salaries, payroll and settlements are for HR, the Finance Controller (payroll only) and directors. Operations Manager, Project Manager, Site Engineer and HSE Officer see the operational HR screens — attendance, man-hours, tasks, certifications — and no pay.
  • Whoever orders does not receive, and whoever receives does not inspect. Procurement raises enquiries and orders; the Storekeeper receives and issues; QA/QC passes or fails deliveries.
  • HR prepares the payroll run; the Finance Controller approves it. Finance / Accounts can no longer change Finance Settings, Tally or Corporate Tax.

The Site Timekeeper is a new built-in role with Attendance & Muster and nothing else.

Changed for BAT

Setting up the testers found six acceptance checks given to a role that could not do them — each tester would have recorded a failure that was really a missing permission. Decided on 21 September 2026:

  • Accounts issues invoices. Finance / Accounts has Approve on Invoices: issuing posts the invoice and gives it its number. (FIN-02, FIN-03, FIN-07)
  • The estimator issues approved quotations and records the order. Estimation / Sales Engineer has Approve on Quotations. The Operations Manager and a Director must still approve the quotation first. (EST-09, EST-11)
  • The Finance Controller locks the books. Finance → Setup → Lock the books, for anyone with Approve on Finance Settings. A future date is refused and every change is in the audit log. (FC-03)
